One contract moves the price

The average hand sums to 16, so a hand's edge is its sum minus 16. Each contract moves the price one point, so the size of a trade tells the pit what a sealed hand knows.

Sealed hands until the bell

MagicBlock VRF deals each seat two cards, 1 to 15, inside a Private Ephemeral Rollup, a TEE. Permissions make them readable by their owner only. At the bell the hands flip on-chain.

One round in four steps

Each step names what lands on-chain.

  1. 00000000

    delegate · request VRF

    Deal the hands

    The table moves to the Rollup and a VRF request deals eight cards.

  2. zero-fee trades

    Trade for 90 seconds

    Every trade clears against the market maker, with one wallet popup each (none in Express mode).

  3. commit · undelegate

    Flip the hands

    At the bell the hidden sum draws across the chart and the round returns to Solana.

  4. 0.01 SOLper seat

    claim

    Claim the 0.01 SOL pot

    The best trader claims the pot on Solana, paid in devnet SOL.
